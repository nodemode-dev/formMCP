# Credits for the demo media

Every picture in the demo is the Form family's own work, taken from the sibling repositories in the same organisation (`nodemode-dev`). No stock photo, third-party image, music or video is used. The licence column is the licence of the source repository, read from its `LICENSE` file on the day of the pass (2026-11-07); the art is the owner's own (NodeAgentc, Inc. and nodemode-ai).

Changes made to every file: the original was only downscaled (never enlarged) and re-encoded. App icons: 256 px PNG to 112 px PNG. Window screenshots: 3200 x 2000, 2560 x 1440 or 2400 x 1500 to 960 px wide, lossy WebP at quality 80, aspect ratio kept. Nothing was cropped, edited or recoloured.
