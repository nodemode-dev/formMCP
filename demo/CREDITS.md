# Credits for the demo media

Every picture in the demo is the Form family's own work, taken from the sibling repositories in the same organisation (`nodemode-dev`), plus the photographs, footage, recordings and documents that appear inside nine of the window screenshots, each credited below to its own source. The licence column of the icons and the three formMCP views is the licence of the source repository, read from its `LICENSE` file on the day of the pass (2026-11-07); that art is the owner's own (NodeAgentc, Inc. and nodemode-ai). The nine sibling window screenshots are the verified demo screenshots of each app (not the docs hero captures, which show photographs whose source and licence cannot be verified), and the media visible in each is listed with its own licence under the screenshot table.

Changes made to every file: the original was only downscaled (never enlarged) and re-encoded. App icons: 256 px PNG to 112 px PNG. Window screenshots: 1440 to 1920 px wide PNGs (or the 3200 x 2000 and 2400 x 1500 formMCP views) to 960 px wide, lossy WebP at quality 80, aspect ratio kept. Nothing was cropped, edited or recoloured.
