# Credits for demo 2 (connecting agents)

Every picture in this demo is the Form family's own work, taken from the sibling repositories in the same organisation (`nodemode-dev`). Nothing else is used: no photographs, footage, audio or fonts. The licence column is the licence of the source repository, read from its `LICENSE`. formMCP is GPL-3.0, so GPL-3.0 sibling art may ship here.

Changes made to the icons: the original 256 px PNG was only downscaled (never enlarged) to 112 px and re-encoded as PNG. Nothing was cropped, edited or recoloured. The files are the same ones demo 1 ships, copied unchanged (checked with `cmp`, and against the sources downscaled again). The window mark is formMCP's own 128 px file, used as it is.
